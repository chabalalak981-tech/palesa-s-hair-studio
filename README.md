## Project Overview
Palesa's Hair Studio is a hair and beauty business that provides professional hair services to clients. The purpose of this website is to create an attractive 
and user-friendly online platform where customers can learn more about the salon, view the services offered, explore hairstyles and contact the business.
## Website Goals and Objectives 
Goals:
Create a professional online presence for Palesa's Hair Studio.
Make information about the salon easily accessible to customers.
Showcase the different hairstyles and services offered.
Make it easier for customers to contact the salon.
Objectives:
Develop a simple and user-friendly website.
Provide clear information about the salon and its services.
Display attractive images of hairstyles and hair servives.
Include an enquiry/contact form.
Ensure that the website is easy to navigate.
## Key Features and Functionality
The website will include:
Home page- Welcome message and attractive salon imagery.
About Us page- Information about Palesa's Hair Studio.
Services page- Hair services and descriptions.
Contact page- Contact information and location.
Enquiries form- Allows customers to submit enquiries.
Navigation menu- Allows users to move between pages.

Day 6 Finalize the website and submit
## Part 1 Details
Part 1 will focus on planning and developing the basic structure of the website. This includes creating the website pages, navigation structures, wireframes, sitemap and basic HTML content.

Goals: 
Create a professional online presence for Celebrate With Us.
Showcase different types of events and services.

Provide customers with information about the business.
Objections:

Include an enquiry/contact form.
